## Questions and predeclared interpretation

Primary: does search improve mean oriented singleton return against the specified
trained pair on each of the three fixed lineages? Three one-sided family95%
Bonferroni Student-t lower bounds, each required above0.

Separate safeguard: does each lineage avoid more than5BB/100 loss against
check-call/pot-pressure? Three independently adjusted family95% one-sided lower
bounds, each required above−5. This margin is a proposed full-hand tolerance for
owner approval, not a margin chosen to pass a particular observed mean. The two
families separately have nominal95% coverage; there is no one overall95% family.

A limited declared positive verdict requires all six lower-bound gates. Any
unmet gate can be inconclusive, not proof of harm. Family upper bound below−5
flags clear material harm separately; publish per-lineage outcomes regardless of
the joint verdict. Never add hands until a bound becomes positive. Publish the
same tail selection, variation shares, recorded decision examples, search/seat/
lookup/quality/off-tree coverage and complete cost as the screen.

This is **not broad scripted acceptance**: random/TAG, pressure/pressure,
trained/pressure and no-intervention check-call controls do not receive new
confirmatory hands. Their original adverse/uncertain evidence remains linked,
not replaced by a passed narrow safeguard. No release adoption, general Nash,
true-belief or safe-resolving claim follows from this design.
